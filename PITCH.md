# Product OS - Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: The Market Problem

### Who has this problem?
**Product Managers at B2B SaaS companies** (teams of 5-50 PMs)

Specifically:
- Mid-market and enterprise software companies
- Teams shipping 10-50 features per quarter
- Companies with OKRs, customer feedback tools, and roadmap pressure

### Market Size
- **180,000+** B2B SaaS companies globally
- **500,000+** product managers in the US alone
- **$15B** spent annually on product management tools (Aha!, Productboard, Jira, etc.)
- **$3.2B** PLG (Product-Led Growth) tool market growing 25% YoY

### How they solve it today (and why it sucks)
| Current Approach | Pain Point |
|-----------------|------------|
| Manual PRD writing in Docs/Notion | Takes 4-8 hours per feature, inconsistent quality |
| Spreadsheets for prioritization | Disconnected from customer voice, gut-feel decisions |
| Meetings with stakeholders | Slow feedback loops, 2-week cycles for alignment |
| AI chat tools (ChatGPT) | No company context, no OKR awareness, generic output |

**The gap:** PMs spend 60% of their time on documentation and alignment, not actual product thinking.

## Slide 2: Your Solution

### One sentence
**Product OS is an agentic PM platform that validates ideas against your OKRs and customer feedback, then generates spec-ready documentation in minutes—not days.**

### Why 10x better?

| Generic AI Tools | Product OS |
|-----------------|------------|
| You provide all context every time | Pre-loaded with your OKRs, customer feedback, company strategy |
| Single-turn Q&A | Multi-agent workflow (Discovery → Strategy → Spec → GTM) |
