# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Product OS to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone. Contributors, maintainers, and community members should be able to participate without harassment or hostility.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent and stay curious
- Give feedback about behavior and code, not about people
- Help keep discussions accessible and on-topic

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or refusing moderator direction

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other community discussions related to this repository.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email [hello@andreaswissel.com](mailto:hello@andreaswissel.com).

Please include:

- what happened
- where it happened
- any relevant links or screenshots

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or ban participants whose behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement decisions will aim to protect the project and its contributors.
